Manage your employee payroll records.

Generated worked time and leave lines are limited to the overlap between
the payslip period and the employee version's effective start and end dates.
Day boundaries follow the resource calendar's timezone. A version outside
the payslip period produces an explicit zero WORK100 line.

This module is a backport from Odoo SA and as such, it is not included
in the OCA CLA. That means we do not have a copy of the copyright on it
like all other OCA modules.
